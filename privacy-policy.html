Song Discovery Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Song Discovery (“the App”) helps you discover music by searching songs, exploring vibes, identifying songs playing nearby, listening to short previews, and opening tracks in Apple Music or Spotify. This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

Summary: Song Discovery does not require an account and does not sell your data. Search queries you enter are sent over the internet to music catalog services so the App can show results. The free version may show ads through Google AdMob on discovery screens. You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

Information We Collect

The App is designed to collect as little information as possible.

  • Search text you type — such as song titles, artist names, or vibe descriptions — is sent to external music services to retrieve search results and recommendations.
  • Song metadata for recommendations — title, artist, genre, and related catalog fields may be sent to Last.fm and (if configured) Google Gemini so the App can suggest similar tracks by listening data and feel.
  • Microphone audio (optional) — only when you tap Identify. Short ambient audio is processed on-device / via Apple’s ShazamKit to match a song. The App does not keep microphone recordings.
  • Technical data needed for network requests — such as your IP address — may be processed by those third-party services when your device contacts them. The App does not store this information on its own servers because the App has no backend servers.
  • Cached media — album artwork and audio preview files may be temporarily cached on your device by the App or iOS to improve performance.

The App does not collect:

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, or camera data
  • Persistent microphone recordings (audio is used only for live identification)
  • Health, financial, or government ID information

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob may collect device and advertising-related data (such as IP address and ad interaction) according to Google’s Privacy Policy. You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How the App Uses Information

Information is used only to operate the App, including:

  • Searching for songs and artists
  • Identifying songs playing nearby (when you choose Identify)
  • Generating similar-song and vibe-based recommendations
  • Loading album artwork and playing 30-second song previews when available
  • Opening selected songs in Apple Music or Spotify
  • Creating playlists in Apple Music or Spotify when you choose Export

Third-Party Services

The App communicates with third-party services to provide its features, including:

  • Apple iTunes Search API — song search, metadata, artwork, and preview URLs
  • Deezer API — related-artist data used for recommendations
  • Last.fm API — listener-based similar tracks, artists, and tagged top tracks used for recommendations
  • Google Gemini API — optional; used only to infer mood/feel and re-rank recommendation candidates when you add an API key
  • Apple Music / Media Library — when you open songs or export a playlist to Apple Music
  • ShazamKit — when you use Identify to match ambient audio to a song
  • Spotify Accounts & Web API — when you sign in and export a playlist to Spotify
  • Google AdMob — banner and occasional full-screen ads on discovery tabs; may use device/ad identifiers for ad delivery and measurement

These services are operated by third parties and have their own privacy policies. We do not control how they process data. Please review Apple’s, Deezer’s, Last.fm’s, Spotify’s, and (if used) Google’s privacy policies for more information.

If you connect Spotify, the App stores Spotify access and refresh tokens on your device only so it can create playlists on your behalf. You can sign out in Settings at any time.

Data Storage and Retention

The App stores some data locally on your device only, such as cached artwork, playlists you create, streaming preferences, and (if you connect Spotify) Spotify tokens needed for playlist export. This local data is not transmitted to the developer. You can remove the App from your device to delete locally stored App data.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

  • You choose what to search for in the App.
  • Song identification is optional and uses the microphone only when you tap Identify. You can deny or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings.
  • Opening Apple Music or Spotify is optional and happens only when you choose to.
  • Connecting Spotify for playlist export is optional; you can sign out in Settings.
  • You can turn off ads in Settings → Support the App (for your device only).
  • You can stop using the App or delete it at any time.

Security

Network communication uses standard HTTPS where supported by the services the App contacts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the App is designed to minimize the amount of data involved.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
