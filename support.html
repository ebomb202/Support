Frequently Asked Questions

Search & discovery

  • Song search isn’t returning results — Check your internet connection and try a simpler query (song title or artist). Some obscure tracks may not appear in the catalog.
  • Similar songs feel off — Recommendations use iTunes, Deezer, Last.fm, and optional AI feel matching. Results vary by how much catalog data exists for that artist.
  • Identify (Shazam) isn’t working — Allow microphone access in iOS Settings → Song Discovery → Microphone, then try again in a quieter environment.

Apple Music & Spotify

  • Open in Apple Music doesn’t work — Make sure Apple Music is installed and you’re signed in with an Apple ID.
  • Spotify export — Sign in under Settings → Spotify Playlist Export. In the Spotify Developer Dashboard, your redirect URI must be songdiscovery://spotify-callback.
  • Change default music app — Go to Settings → Open Songs In and pick Apple Music or Spotify.

Ads & Remove Ads purchase

  • Remove Ads — One-time purchase in Settings → Support the App. It removes banner and full-screen ads on all discovery tabs.
  • Restore purchase — Tap Restore Purchases in Settings using the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Tracking permission — iOS may ask if ads can track you across apps. You can decline and still use Song Discovery; ads may be less personalized.

Playlists & previews

  • 30-second previews — Not every track has a preview available in the catalog.
  • Playlists — Playlists are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes locally stored playlists.

Privacy

Song Discovery does not require an account. Search text is sent to music services to fetch results. Optional ads are served by Google AdMob. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
